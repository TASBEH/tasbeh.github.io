Thesis & Research

Multi-Label Classification of Big Five Personality Traits from Bangla Social Media Text Using Deep Learning

Undergraduate Thesis, CUET · 2026 · GitHub repository
0.8897Macro F1
0.8882Micro F1
0.7482Subset Accuracy
0.9741Mean ROC-AUC
  • Built BMPC, the Bangla Multi-label Personality Corpus: 8,232 public posts from YouTube, Facebook and Reddit, labelled across all five OCEAN traits by three annotators.
  • Designed TAP-BERT (Trait-Aware Personality BERT): BanglaBERT encoder with five trait-specific attention queries for separate trait representations.
  • Combined trait attention with FGM adversarial training, multi-sample dropout, label smoothing, three-seed ensembling and per-trait threshold optimisation.
  • Outperformed vanilla BanglaBERT (0.8739 Macro F1) on the held-out test split.
  • Mean pairwise Cohen's kappa of 0.7538 via independent annotation, majority review and psychology-domain adjudication.
